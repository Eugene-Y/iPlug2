# IPlugControls tooltip (the horizontal ISVGSliderControl's "ISVGSlider H"): shows below the pointer after a rest,
# hides on leaving the editor, shows again. Renderer-independent; written for the Skia builds (T-018), whose tooltip
# is drawn in software.
move 577 -40
wait 1
shot t00-no-tooltip
move 577 277
wait 1.5
shot t01-tooltip
expect-change t00-no-tooltip t01-tooltip 570 292 110 30
move 577 -40
wait 0.5
shot t02-gone
expect-same t00-no-tooltip t02-gone 570 292 110 30
move 577 277
wait 1.5
shot t03-tooltip-again
expect-change t02-gone t03-tooltip-again 570 292 110 30
alive __PROC__
